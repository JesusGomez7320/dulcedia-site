The people behind the films

Sarah & Jesus

We are a husband-and-wife creative duo with more than five years of experience each, separately photographing and filming weddings before bringing our strengths together as Dulce Día Cine.

Jesus worked in film and television commercials, refining his eye for mood, movement, sound, and story. He is also a full-time editor and videographer for local San Clemente companies, bringing that production discipline to every wedding film.

Sarah is a photographer who connects easily with all kinds of personalities. Her personable approach helps couples feel comfortable, trust themselves, and enjoy being in front of the camera. She is creative with composition and knows how to translate your ideas into photographs that feel like you.

Working together

Direction when you want it. Space when you need it.

We guide gently, notice the in-between moments, and make room for real reactions. Our documentary approach is natural and observant, with an editorial finish that keeps the final film and photographs feeling intentional.

The result is a calm experience for you and a fuller story for the people you love: the quiet nerves, the loud laughter, the hands reaching for each other, and the celebration that happens around the planned timeline.
