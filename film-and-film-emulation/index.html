From negative to digital keepsake

Thoughtful film scanning & editing.

The work continues after the shutter clicks. Our scanning and editing process is designed to translate the detail, color, and texture of your negatives into high-quality digital photographs.

We pay attention to scan sharpness, exposure, color balance, and visible dust, then refine the images while preserving the character that made you choose film. The goal is a cohesive gallery with natural skin tones, considered contrast, and grain that feels like part of the photograph.

We prepare high-resolution digital files for viewing, sharing, and printing. Scan size and export settings are chosen with the image and intended use in mind; if you’re planning a large print or album, tell us so we can discuss the right delivery size for it.

Find your film-emulation style

Professional tools. Intentional choices.

Crafted in DaVinci Resolve.

Beautiful film emulation begins with the original capture. We shoot our wedding footage in RAW and prioritize the highest-quality source files, giving us a rich starting point for accurate color, natural skin tones, and a carefully controlled film-inspired finish.

Preserving that image information gives us more flexibility to refine exposure, balance color, and shape highlights and shadows before adding the emulation. From a clean, polished grade to a more expressive vintage look, the finish is built on a high-quality foundation.

We use DaVinci Resolve, professional film-emulation tools, and industry-standard color-grading techniques to build a considered look. Exposure and white balance come first, followed by shot matching, tonal shaping, and the film-inspired color response.

Grain, highlight bloom, and other texture choices are adjusted to suit the footage and the style you choose. We consider how the image feels in motion as well as in a still frame, keeping skin tones and the emotion of your day at the center of the grade.

The finishing process is about consistency: a look that holds together across changing light, different scenes, and the complete story. Tell us how subtle or nostalgic you want it to feel, and we’ll discuss the scope and price of your video-emulation package.

Film photography & emulation FAQs

What is the difference between film photography and film emulation?

Real film photography exposes an image onto physical film, which is developed and scanned. Film emulation starts with a digital photo or video and uses editing to create film-inspired color, contrast, and texture. We offer both approaches and can help you decide which fits your priorities.

Can I choose Portra 400, Ektar, or another film stock?

Yes. Tell us the stock or look you have in mind. We’ll discuss suitability, availability, and pricing. If you’re undecided, we can help choose an approach based on your setting, visual preferences, and budget.

Is photo film emulation really included at no extra cost?

Yes. Film-emulation styling for digital photos is available at no additional cost with your photography package. Let us know your preferred direction when we plan your coverage. Real film photography and video film-emulation packages are priced separately.

Are your Super 8, 16mm, and 35mm video looks shot on film?

Our video-emulation packages use digital footage with a finish inspired by those film formats. They give you a way to explore a nostalgic or cinematic aesthetic without paying for physical motion-picture film capture and processing.

Will I receive digital copies of my real-film photographs?

Yes. Film photographs are scanned and edited for digital delivery. We prepare high-resolution files and can discuss scan dimensions and print needs as part of your package.

Do you offer film wedding photography in Orange County and San Clemente?

We welcome inquiries for weddings and engagements in Orange County, San Clemente, and across Southern California. Share your date and location so we can discuss availability and coverage.
